# Privacy Policy for Gastos

_Last updated: October 2026_

Gastos is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## What we collect

Nothing. Gastos does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter (expenses, notes, categories, budgets and settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to any server, and we have no access to it.

## Third parties

Gastos has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

## Exporting your data

If you use "Export as CSV", the file goes wherever you choose to share it (for example, to an email app). That is your choice, and we do not receive it.

## Deleting your data

You can delete expenses inside the app ("Erase all expenses"), or remove all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone's settings.

## Children

Gastos does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
